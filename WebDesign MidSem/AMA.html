Accra Metropolitan Assembly (AMA)

Working Together for a Better Accra

Welcome to the AMA Community Information Board. This platform is designed to keep residents informed about important community updates, public services, local events, sanitation programs, safety announcements, and development projects within Accra. Our goal is to encourage active community participation and improve communication between the Assembly and residents for a cleaner, safer, and better city.
